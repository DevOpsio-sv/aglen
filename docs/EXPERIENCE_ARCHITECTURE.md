# Experience Architecture — the Lukovit Karst

**Phase 3 — System Architecture (1 of 4).** The experience constitution of the
Aglen / "Unlocking Bulgaria" project.
Companion to the ten knowledge-architecture documents, and their opposite number:
those answer *what a person should know*; this one answers *how a person should
feel their way into knowing it*.

Reads with `TOPICAL_AUTHORITY_MAP.md` (the subject and the inventory),
`KNOWLEDGE_GRAPH.md` (the substrate every experience runs on),
`CONTENT_HIERARCHY.md` (where things live), `SEARCH_INTENT_MAP.md` (why people
arrive), `INTERNAL_LINKING_GRAPH.md` (the edges the loops travel),
`EEAT_STRATEGY.md` (the claim ledger that makes the AI honest) and the
`5_YEAR_SEO_ROADMAP.md` (when any of this can exist).

> **The one question this document answers:** how should a human progressively
> **experience, discover and emotionally connect with** the Lukovit Karst —
> not what should they know, and not how should the site rank.

Verification legend, used throughout, identical to the sibling documents:

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ | Present in this repository, already built |
| 🟢 | Externally verifiable now (Wikidata / Wikipedia / register) |
| 🔶 | Believed true, must be sourced before it is experienced as fact |
| ⬜ | Unknown; needs field work before it can be part of the experience |
| 🔴 | Known gap where the experience currently breaks |

## 1. Experience Philosophy

### 1.1 Why this experience exists

Almost nobody will arrive here looking for the Lukovit Karst. They will arrive
looking for the Eyes of God — Проходна — because it is the one thing in this
region a search engine already knows. The karst, the arch called Дупката, the
silent cave people of the legend, the writer born in the only Bulgarian village
that begins with "Ъ": none of these are being searched for, because a person
cannot look for a thing they do not yet know is there.

The purpose of this experience is therefore not to *satisfy* a demand. It is to
**convert a narrow, famous demand into a wide, unfamiliar connection.** A person
comes for a cave with two holes in its roof and leaves having met a landscape,
a deep-time, a literature and a set of names that exist in no other place on the
internet. That conversion is the entire product. Everything else is machinery.

### 1.2 How it differs from a tourism website

A tourism website is an **answer surface**. It receives a question — where to
sleep, what to see, how to get there — and returns the shortest correct answer,
so the visitor can close the tab and go. Its success metric is the speed with
which the person leaves.

This is the inverse. Its success metric is the number of times a person who came
for one thing chose, unprompted, to look at a second thing they had never heard
of. A tourism site optimises for *exit*; an exploration optimises for *the next
step inward*.

| Tourism website | This experience |
|---|---|
| Answers a question the visitor already has | Provokes questions the visitor did not know to ask |
| Sells a place | Reveals a place |
| Descriptions ("beautiful canyon") | Observations ("the east wall is in shadow until ~09:30 in October") |
| The village is the product | The village is the narrator; the karst is the subject |
| Content is complete and closed | Content is honest and open — states its own unknowns |
| Wants the booking | Wants the return, the memory, the contribution |
| Trust is asserted by tone | Trust is earned by showing sources and admitting gaps |

The single most important line the current site already holds — `guides.ts`
refusing to publish a distance it has not measured (✅) — is not an SEO tactic.
It is an *experiential* one. A person who catches a site telling the truth about
what it does not know will believe it about what it does. That is the emotional
foundation the whole architecture stands on.

### 1.3 Why it is closer to exploration than browsing

Browsing is lateral: a person moves across a catalogue of comparable items,
choosing. Exploration is inward and directional: a person follows a thread,
and each thing found changes what the next thing means. The karst is not a
catalogue. It is a connected world — the same Lower-Cretaceous limestone under
Проходна is under Дупката (🔶, must be sourced before it can carry the reader);
the abandoned settlement Селището explains why the present village sits where it
does; the legend of the Двукраките сенки and the Late Neolithic flint workshops
are, uncomfortably, describing the same terraces from two directions.

An experience built on a graph (`KNOWLEDGE_GRAPH.md`) can move a person **along
true relations** instead of across a menu. That is the difference this document
is written to protect: the reader should never feel they are choosing from a
list. They should feel they are following something that keeps turning out to be
connected to the last thing.

## 2. Experience Layers

Nine layers. A person does not climb them in order and does not reach them all;
most visits touch three or four. A layer is defined by *what it does for the
human*, the **trigger** that admits them, and the **carry** — the specific thing
that hands them to a deeper layer. A layer with no carry is a dead end, and a
dead end is where an exploration becomes a browse.

| # | Layer | What it does for the human | Entry trigger | Carry to next |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L0 | **Orientation** | Tells them, in one breath, what world they have entered and that it is real | Arrival from search, a link, an AR quest, a photo | A single true, strange fact that cannot be un-known |
| L1 | **Discovery** | Lets them find a thing they did not know existed | The strange fact provokes "what else" | A named entity with a photograph and a location |
| L2 | **Wonder** | Makes the thing feel larger than itself | Seeing the thing rendered specifically, not generically | A question the thing raises but does not answer |
| L3 | **Understanding** | Answers the question with a sourced fact and opens two more | Curiosity earned in L2 | An edge to a second entity along a true relation |
| L4 | **Planning** | Turns "I want to see this" into "I can see this on Saturday" | Desire to be there in person | A route, a distance, a season, a place to stay |
| L5 | **Participation** | Lets them act — walk, quest, photograph, attend | Being physically present or committing to be | An on-the-ground prompt tied to an entity |
| L6 | **Contribution** | Lets them add what they know or correct what is wrong | Recognising a gap they can fill | A credited claim entering the ledger |
| L7 | **Memory** | Holds what they experienced so it stays theirs | The visit ends | A reason the experience is unfinished |
| L8 | **Return** | Brings them back to a changed version of the same place | A season turns, a quest updates, a gap they reported gets filled | Back to L1, but deeper |

### 2.1 L0 — Orientation

**Does:** in the first fifteen seconds, establishes that this is a real,
specific, slightly uncanny place and not a template. The orientation is not a
value proposition; it is a *fact that reorganises the visitor's expectations*.
The strongest available is the "Ъ" fact — the only settlement in Bulgaria whose
name begins with "Ъ" (✅, currently buried in a stats tile). It is memorable,
unique, checkable, and it signals immediately that this place has edges a
template would have sanded off.

**Trigger:** any arrival. **Carry:** the strange fact must itself be a door —
"the only village starting with Ъ" leads to "why?", which is the etymology
dispute (charcoal-burners vs. the Иглените скали rock needles, ✅), which is L1.
An orientation that states the fact but does not open it has wasted the only
moment when attention is guaranteed.

### 2.2 L1 — Discovery

**Does:** produces the encounter with an unfamiliar entity — Дупката, Слончето,
Рачков вир, the Въловата дупка cave. This is the layer the current site cannot
enter at all, because **it has no entities to discover** (`KNOWLEDGE_GRAPH.md`
§0): the named places are mentions inside a page about something else, so there
is nothing to *arrive at*.

**Trigger:** "what else is here." **Carry:** a discovered entity must carry a
photograph with a capture date, a location, and one specific observation — the
minimum that makes it feel found rather than listed.

### 2.3 L2 — Wonder

**Does:** the emotional peak of a first visit. It is produced by *specificity*,
never by adjectives. "Great for photography" produces nothing. "The arch is lit
from 07:15; the canyon's east wall stays in shadow until ~09:30 in October"
produces the feeling of a real thing standing in real light, which is wonder.
The beautiful-places guide already contains the site's only real writing of this
kind (✅, `guides.ts` photography notes).

**Trigger:** an entity rendered concretely. **Carry:** wonder must leave a
*question* behind — why is there an arch here at all? — or it dissipates. The
question is the fuel for L3.

### 2.4 L3 — Understanding

**Does:** answers the question wonder raised, with a sourced claim, and in
answering opens an edge to a second entity. "There is an arch here because the
Lower-Cretaceous limestone is the same formation that Проходна is cut into"
(🔶) does two things at once: it satisfies, and it hands the reader Проходна —
or, travelling the other way, hands the Проходна visitor Дупката. This is the
**authority-transfer edge** of `TOPICAL_AUTHORITY_MAP.md` §0, experienced from
the inside as *the pleasure of a connection*.

**Trigger:** earned curiosity. **Carry:** every understanding must end on an
edge, never on a full stop.

### 2.5 L4 — Planning

**Does:** converts connection into a plan. This is where the experience meets the
body: a real road distance, a drive time, a season, a route with a length, a
place to sleep. It is the layer most completely broken today — 🔴 **there is no
verified distance or drive time anywhere on the site** (`CONTENT_GAP_ANALYSIS.md`
B1), so the person who has been moved to want to come cannot find out how.

**Trigger:** "I want to be there." **Carry:** a plan carries to participation
only if it is trustworthy; a guessed distance that turns out wrong destroys the
trust every earlier layer built.

### 2.6 L5 — Participation

**Does:** the person acts — walks a route, plays an AR quest with Пазителят (✅),
photographs Дупката at 07:15, attends the Панаир on 28 August (✅). This is the
only layer that happens partly off the screen, and the experience's job is to be
*present but silent* there: a prompt at the trailhead, a quest node at the cave,
not a feed demanding attention while someone is standing in a canyon.

**Trigger:** physical presence. **Carry:** participation produces something —
a photograph, a completed quest, a thing seen — that becomes the seed of memory
and, sometimes, contribution.

### 2.7 L6 — Contribution

**Does:** lets the visitor become a source. Someone who grew up here knows where
Слончето actually is; someone who walked the path from the church in July knows
it was overgrown. The experience must have a mouth for this — a way to say "you
know something we don't; tell us and we will credit you" (`EEAT_STRATEGY.md` §7).
🔴 The policy exists; the mechanism does not (`SEARCH_INTENT_MAP.md` N7).

**Trigger:** recognising a fillable gap — which only exists because the site
*shows its gaps* (§5). **Carry:** a contribution enters as a credited claim, and
the contributor now has a stake, which is the strongest possible reason to
return.

### 2.8 L7 — Memory

**Does:** holds the experience after the tab closes and the visit ends. Memory is
not a feature; it is the residue of specificity. Nobody remembers "a beautiful
canyon in Bulgaria." People remember "the arch that is lit before the canyon
floor is," "the cave where the silent people lit smokeless fires," "the only
village that starts with Ъ." The experience is *designed to be quotable to a
friend*, because a memory retold is a memory kept.

**Trigger:** the visit ends. **Carry:** memory must contain an *unfinished
edge* — a place not reached, a season not seen, a legend half-told — so that the
memory itself is a reason to return.

### 2.9 L8 — Return

**Does:** brings the person back to a version of the place that has changed since
they left — a new season page, a quest chapter, a gap they reported now filled,
a photograph of the same viewpoint one year on. Return is where a website becomes
a relationship.

**Trigger:** a change the person is told about, or a season they were promised.
**Carry:** return re-enters at L1, but the person is no longer a stranger, so
discovery is deeper and faster. This is the loop that compounds.

## 3. Emotional Journey

The arc, in feelings rather than pages:

```
Curiosity → Discovery → Mystery → Reward → Connection → Belonging → Return
   L0          L1         L2        L3         L3→L4        L6          L8
    │           │          │         │           │            │          │
 "only Ъ"   "Дупката    "why is  "same       "I could    "I know    "the arch
  village"   exists"    an arch   limestone   stand       where      in winter"
                        here?"    as Проходна" there"     Слончето is"
```

### 3.1 The arc, stage by stage

| Stage | Feeling | Produced by | The entity that carries it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Curiosity** | "wait, what?" | one true strange fact | the "Ъ" village; the Eyes of God |
| **Discovery** | "I didn't know this was here" | an entity that exists nowhere else | Дупката, Въловата дупка, Рачков вир |
| **Mystery** | "why? / how?" | specificity that raises a question | the arch; the smokeless fires of the Двукраките сенки |
| **Reward** | "oh — *that's* why" | a sourced answer that also connects | same formation as Проходна; flint workshops under the legend |
| **Connection** | "these things are one place" | edges along true relations | Селището → Ъглен; Кунев → the village |
| **Belonging** | "this is partly mine now" | contribution, or a plan committed to | a credited correction; the Панаир on 28 August |
| **Desire to return** | "I want to see the rest" | an honest, visible unfinished edge | the winter arch; the unreached path; the season not seen |

### 3.2 Where the arc breaks

Stated because a design review should include its own objections. Each break is a
point where a real person, today, stops feeling and starts leaving.

| Break | Where | Why the feeling dies | Fix (experience-level) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Curiosity has nowhere to go** | after L0 | The strange fact is stated but not a door; the "Ъ" fact sits in a stats tile with no path onward (✅ present, 🔴 inert) | Every orientation fact must open onto an entity |
| **Nothing to discover** | L1 | The site has no entity pages; the named places are mentions, so discovery cannot begin (🔴, `KNOWLEDGE_GRAPH.md` §0) | Give every named place a destination of its own |
| **Wonder replaced by adjective** | L2 | "spring and autumn are strongest for photography" is true of everywhere, so it moves no one (`SEARCH_INTENT_MAP.md` §0) | Replace every adjective with an observation carrying a number |
| **Reward with no connection** | L3 | A fact that ends on a full stop instead of an edge; the ~4000 words of rare history end inside a modal with no way out (🔴) | Every answer ends on a true relation to a second entity |
| **The plan cannot be made** | L4 | No distance, no drive time, no route length — desire to visit meets a blank (🔴, B1) | One driving day, one walking day; the cheapest emotional repair on the roadmap |
| **The wall of near-duplicates** | anywhere | 25 landing pages render the same three bodies under different names; the reader feels the seam and the trust cracks | Retire them; one real thing beats eighteen labels |
| **No mouth to answer back** | L6 | Belonging is impossible when the person cannot contribute; the site talks and never listens (🔴, N7) | A visible, credited contribution route |
| **No reason to return** | L7→L8 | Memory holds nothing unfinished; the site presents itself as complete | Show the seams — the unreached, the unseen, the not-yet-known |

The through-line of every break is the same: **the current site tries to appear
finished, and a finished thing gives no reason to keep exploring.** The
architecture's central emotional move is to make incompleteness a feature the
visitor can feel and act on.

## 4. Experience Loops

Loops are the engine. A layer describes how a person goes deeper once; a loop is
a *reusable path* that returns the person to a new beginning with more than they
had. Every loop below is drawn as arrows and grounded in named entities. A loop
is valid only if each arrow is a **true relation in the graph** — never a
convenience link (`INTERNAL_LINKING_GRAPH.md` §1).

### 4.1 The master loop (the one every other specialises)

```
Photo → Question → Story → Place → Nearby place → Route → Business → Memory → Next place
                                                                                    │
                                                                                    └──┐
        ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
        ▼
     (a new Photo, of a place the reader had never heard of when they arrived)
```

Worked example, entirely in real entities:

```
Photo of an arch over a river
  → "what is this?"            (Question)
  → Дупката, a limestone arch  (Place)                                  E1
  → "why is there an arch?"    (Question again)
  → same Lower-Cretaceous formation as Проходна  (Story / Understanding) 🔶
  → Проходна, the Eyes of God  (Nearby place, 19.8 km straight-line)     E5
  → the canyon walk that passes the arch  (Route)                        ⬜ needs GPX
  → a guest house on the Vit reach  (Business)                           ✅ directory
  → "the arch is lit at 07:15"  (Memory — a thing to come back for)
  → Слончето, the next rock figure the reader now wants to find  (Next place) E1
```

The person entered searching for a cave they'd heard of and left with a rock
arch, a rock figure, a river reach, a walk and a bed — none of which they knew
existed. That is the loop doing its only job.

### 4.2 The history loop

```
"only village starting with Ъ"  (Orientation fact)
  → "why Ъ?"  (etymology dispute — charcoal-burners vs. Иглените скали)  ✅
  → Селището / Старо село, the abandoned earlier settlement  (Place)      E1
  → "why did they move?"
  → Никополски санджак tax registers + the Кърджалии raids  (Understanding) ✅
  → Св. Архангел Михаил, the 1888 church built for the raid dead  (Place)  E1
  → Трифон Кунев, the writer born here in 1880  (Person)                   E4 🟢
  → out to Wikidata, and back  (Connection to the known graph)
  → "who else remembers this?"  → Contribution loop (§4.6)
```

This loop needs no new writing — the text exists in `locales/bg.ts` and is
unreachable (🔴). It is the highest-value loop to build and the cheapest.

### 4.3 The legend loop

```
Legend: "Двукраките сенки" — silent cave people who lit smokeless fires  ✅
  → "is this true?"  (Mystery — the honest answer is *we don't know*)
  → Late Neolithic flint workshops on the terraces  (Understanding)       ✅ E2
     — the legend and the archaeology describe the same ground
  → "what is a flint workshop?"  → the prehistory as an *industrial* site, not a scenic one
  → Въловата дупка / Очилатата, the refuge cave of the tragedy  (Place)   E1
  → "Златната браздичка" — the golden furrow at the river's sharpest bend (Legend)
  → Рачков вир, the river pool at that bend  (Place)                      E1
  → back to a Photo of the bend the reader now wants to stand at
```

Discipline that protects this loop: a legend page **asserts that the legend is
told, never that the legend is true** (`CONTENT_HIERARCHY.md` §2.4). The mystery
is the emotional asset; overclaiming it destroys it. Пазителят, the AR guardian,
is a *modern invention* and must never be mixed into collected folklore.

### 4.4 The season / return loop

```
First visit: Дупката in October, "the arch is lit at 07:15"  (Memory)
  → the page states what the reader did NOT see: the arch in winter mist,
    the river in spring flood, the terraces flowering in May  (visible unfinished edge)
  → a promise: "come back in April; the Vit is highest and the light is longest"
  → [season turns]
  → Return: the April version of the same entity  (L8 → L1, deeper)
  → the reader now photographs the flood they were promised  (Participation)
  → their photograph, dated, can enter the record  (Contribution)
```

This is the loop that turns a one-time visit into a relationship, and it is only
possible because the experience is honest that a single visit is incomplete. It
depends on seasonal observation the site does not yet have (⬜) — but the *edge*
("you did not see the winter arch") can be shown before the winter photograph
exists.

### 4.5 The AI-companion loop (see also §7)

```
Reader asks the companion: "is Дупката hard to reach?"
  → companion answers only from a sourced claim, or says "not yet recorded"  ✅ ledger
  → "what's near it?"
  → companion travels a real graph edge → Проходна (same formation)          🔶
  → "could I see both in a day?"
  → companion composes a plan from claims (distances, season) — never invents  🔴 needs B1
  → "I don't think that path exists any more"
  → companion routes the correction into the ledger  (Contribution)
  → reader has taught the site, and will return to see if it listened
```

The loop's integrity depends entirely on the companion refusing to invent. The
moment it guesses a distance, the loop becomes a chatbot and the trust that took
six layers to build is gone.

### 4.6 The contribution loop

```
Reader sees a stated unknown: "we do not know if swimming at Рачков вир is safe"  ✅ pattern
  → recognises they know something  ("I fish there; the deep pool is below the bend")
  → contribution route accepts it  (🔴 mechanism missing)
  → enters as a claim: method "interview", source = the named reader
  → appears on the page, credited, dated
  → reader returns to see their name on the place they know
  → tells the person who told them the legend to add theirs too  (loop widens)
```

The contribution loop is the only loop that *grows the graph*, and therefore the
only one that makes every other loop richer over time. It is powered by §5's
refusal to hide gaps: a site that looks complete gives no one anything to add.

### 4.7 The school-trip loop

```
Teacher arrives: "field trip, northern Bulgaria, one day"
  → a single ground assembles a whole syllabus:
       geology      → the karst, the Lower-Cretaceous strata                ⬜ needs /karst/
       prehistory   → the flint workshops (industrial prehistory)           ✅ text exists
       literature   → Трифон Кунев, a national writer born in the village    🟢 E4
       history       → the Ottoman registers, the 1888 church                ✅
  → route with a length, safety notes, group logistics  (Planning)          🔴 needs GPX + B1
  → the class walks it  (Participation)
  → the class photographs and the teacher reports what was wrong  (Contribution)
  → next year's class returns to a corrected route  (Return)
```

Bulgarian schools run mandatory excursions; a region 100 km from Sofia with a
literary figure, a karst and a prehistoric industrial site is a complete
field-trip and nobody has assembled it (`SEARCH_INTENT_MAP.md` J4). The loop's
value is that four subjects sit on one piece of ground.

### 4.8 Loop health — what makes a loop real

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Every arrow is a true graph relation | A convenience link is felt as a sales funnel and breaks trust |
| Every loop ends on a *new* entity | A loop that returns to where it started is a browse |
| At least one arrow crosses from famous → local (E5 → E1) | This is the authority-and-attention transfer; a loop that never crosses it never introduces the reader to anything only this site holds |
| No loop passes through a near-duplicate page | The seam is felt; the loop stalls |
| Every loop has an exit to Contribution | A loop with no mouth cannot grow the graph it runs on |

## 5. Information Reveal Strategy

What a person meets immediately, what they must explore to find, what should feel
earned, and what must never be hidden. The reveal strategy is where an
exploration is won or lost, because revealing everything at once is a catalogue
and revealing nothing is a locked door.

### 5.1 Immediately visible (given without cost)

The person has spent no attention yet, so what they meet must be true, strange,
and small.

| Shown at once | Why | State |
|---|---|---|
| One orientation fact that reorganises expectation | It is the only moment attention is free | ✅ "Ъ" fact exists |
| That this is a *real, specific* place | Defends against the "template" reflex | 🔶 undermined by AI-looking imagery |
| What kind of world this is (a karst, narrated from a village) | Sets the frame; the subject is the region | 🔴 site frames itself as a village |
| That the site tells the truth about what it doesn't know | The trust foundation, stated early | ✅ disposition exists, ⬜ not surfaced early |

### 5.2 Requires exploration (found, not given)

The entities themselves — Дупката, Слончето, Въловата дупка, the legends, the
history — are the *reward for looking*, not the shop window. They should be
reachable in ≤3 steps (`CONTENT_HIERARCHY.md` §3) but not pushed. The pleasure of
discovery requires that the thing was found, and a thing shoved into the first
screen cannot be found.

### 5.3 Should feel earned (revealed by depth, not by paywall)

Nothing here is *withheld* — this is not a gamified drip. But some things only
*mean* anything after the reader has done the earlier work:

- The flint workshops mean "industrial prehistory" only after the reader has met
  the legend of the silent fire-makers and asked whether it is true.
- The Кунев birthplace edge means something only after the reader knows the
  village is otherwise unknown.
- The claim ledger (`/sources/`) is dull to a browser and thrilling to a
  researcher who has already decided to trust the site and wants to check it.

Earned reveal is achieved by *sequencing along the loops*, never by locking.

### 5.4 Must NEVER be hidden — the non-negotiables

This is the principle the current site already half-lives and must live fully.
The following are shown with the *same prominence as the good news*, never in a
footnote, never omitted to keep a page clean:

| Never hidden | Because |
|---|---|
| **Safety** — unfenced cliff edges, cave hazards, river danger | A hidden hazard is the one failure that can hurt someone; ✅ already published, correctly |
| **Unknowns** — "we do not know if swimming at Рачков вир is safe; here is who to ask" | The stated unknown outperforms the guess on trust, and it is the mouth that makes contribution possible; ✅ pattern exists, must become a design law |
| **Sources** — every claim's provenance and date | It is the thing a careful person, and an AI, uses to decide whether to repeat you; 🔴 no claim ledger yet |
| **What is a modern invention** — Пазителят is a made character, not folklore | Mixing invented and collected tradition would poison the site's rarest asset |
| **What is straight-line vs. road distance** | The site already refuses to blur this (✅); the reader must feel the honesty, not just benefit from it |

> **The refusal to guess is not a limitation of the experience. It is the
> experience's signature.** Promote it from an editorial habit (`guides.ts`) to a
> visible, repeated, felt pattern. A person who sees the site admit one gap
> believes it about everything else — and, crucially, is handed something to
> contribute.

## 6. Progressive Disclosure

How the experience unfolds over time — within one visit, and across a
relationship. The unit is not the page; it is the *minute* and the *visit*.

### 6.1 Within the first visit

| Moment | What the person should have | Grounded in |
|---|---|---|
| **Minute 1** | One strange true fact and the sense that this is real. Nothing to read yet, one thing to feel. "The only village in Bulgaria that starts with Ъ." | ✅ fact, must lead L0 |
| **Minute 5** | One discovered entity they'd never heard of, rendered specifically enough to raise a question. "There is an arch over the Vit, lit at 07:15." | 🔴 needs entity pages + one observation |
| **Minute 20** | One connection that made two things into one place, and the beginning of a plan. "The arch is the same limestone as the Eyes of God, 19.8 km away, and I could walk the canyon between." | 🔶 formation claim; 🔴 route + distance |
| **End of visit** | One memory quotable to a friend, and one unfinished edge. "The arch I have to go back and see in winter." | depends on the honest incompleteness of §5 |

The current site's failure, stated in this frame: **minute 5 never arrives.**
There is nothing to discover, so the person who felt curiosity at minute 1 has
nowhere to spend it, and leaves at minute 2.

### 6.2 Across the relationship

| Visit | What changes | The mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| **First visit** | Meets one entity, one connection, one memory | The master loop, run once |
| **Second visit** | Recognises the place; goes past the famous entity to a local one they now remember exists; makes a real plan | The season/return loop begins; L8 → L1 deeper |
| **Third visit** | Contributes — corrects a path, adds a name, sends a photograph — and now has a stake | The contribution loop; belonging |
| **Returning visitor** | Comes for the *changes* — new season, filled gap, new quest chapter, the same viewpoint one year on | The site has become a maintained record, not a brochure; this is the compounding position of the `5_YEAR_SEO_ROADMAP.md` |

Progressive disclosure across visits is only possible if the site *changes* and
*says how it changed* — which is why `/corrections/` and `/field-notes/`
(`EEAT_STRATEGY.md` §6) are experiential features, not compliance ones. A person
returns to a thing that is alive.

## 7. AI Experience

AI participates here not as a chatbot bolted to a corner, but as **five faces of
the same companion**, each drawing on the one shared substrate — the claim ledger
and the graph (`EEAT_STRATEGY.md` §2, `KNOWLEDGE_GRAPH.md`). The rule that makes
all five trustworthy is a single sentence:

> **The companion narrates only sourced claims, states its unknowns plainly, and
> never invents. When it does not know, it says who might.**

This is not a safety disclaimer. It is the thing that makes the AI *worth talking
to*, because it inherits the exact property that makes the whole site
trustworthy: it refuses to guess.

| Face | What it does | What it draws on | The line it must not cross |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Guide** | "You're standing at Дупката; the arch is lit now, the canyon floor is not yet" | entity claims + season + light | Never invent a light hour that was not observed |
| **Storyteller** | Tells the legend of the Двукраките сенки *as a legend*, then, if asked, the flint-workshop archaeology beside it | `/story/` + `/history/` claims | Never present a legend as fact; never merge Пазителят with collected folklore |
| **Planner** | Composes a Saturday from Проходна + Iskar–Panega + the Aglen reach with real distances | the distance matrix + season claims | Never state a drive time that has no source (🔴 today it would have to) |
| **Local** | Answers "is the river safe to swim?" with "not known; ask the municipality" and means it | the stated-unknowns in the ledger | Never fill the silence the human site deliberately left |
| **Memory-keeper** | Remembers what this person saw, and tells them what has changed since — the winter arch is now photographed | visit memory + `/corrections/` + `/field-notes/` | Never fabricate a change to manufacture a return |

### 7.1 Why this is an experience and not a feature

A chatbot answers and is forgotten. This companion is *the same voice across the
whole journey* — it orients at L0, discovers with the reader at L1, connects at
L3, plans at L4, walks beside them at L5, takes their correction at L6, and holds
their memory at L7. It is the human's continuity through a graph too large to
browse. The graph is what it knows; the ledger is what keeps it honest; the loops
(§4) are the paths it walks the person along.

### 7.2 The failure the ledger prevents

Without the claim ledger, an AI companion on this site would do what every
tourism chatbot does: confidently invent a drive time, a swimming spot, a cave
access rule — and the first time a visitor drives the invented distance and finds
it wrong, every earlier layer of trust collapses at once. The ledger is not
infrastructure for SEO here; it is **the thing that lets the companion be
honest**, which is the only version of the companion worth building.

## 8. Cross-platform Experience

Website, mobile, AR quests, maps, the AI companion, and future wearables are not
six products. They are **six windows onto one world**, and the world is the graph
(`KNOWLEDGE_GRAPH.md`). The through-line — the thing that makes them continuous
rather than merely consistent — is that **every surface reads and writes the same
entities, claims and edges.** A person moves between them without ever leaving the
Lukovit Karst.

| Surface | What it is best at | What it contributes to the shared world | The entity is the join |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Website** | Depth — understanding, history, sources, the full loops | Reading and reasoning across the whole graph | A `/place/дупката/` is the canonical node |
| **Mobile** | Presence — the person is *here*, now, outside | Context: what is near me, what is lit now | Same node, filtered by GPS proximity |
| **AR quests** ✅ | Play and place — Пазителят at the cave, a puzzle at the sanctuary | Turns standing at an entity into an event | The quest node *is* a graph entity, or it is disconnected |
| **Maps** | Location and route — the graph as geography | Coordinates, GPX, the walk between two nodes | Every mapped point is an entity with a `sameAs` |
| **AI companion** | Continuity and answer — the voice across all of it | Narration, planning, memory, correction | It speaks only in graph claims (§7) |
| **Future wearables** | Ambient presence — the whisper at the trailhead | Hands-free participation while walking | Only possible if the entity carries its own trigger geometry |

### 8.1 The through-line, stated plainly

A visitor reads about Дупката on the website (L3), saves it, drives out with the
mobile map guiding them (L4), plays the AR quest that lives *at* the arch (L5),
photographs it at 07:15, asks the companion what is nearby and is sent to
Проходна along the formation edge (L3 again, in the field), and reports on the
way home that the last 200 m of path was washed out (L6). **Six surfaces, one
continuous experience, because all six were pointing at the same node in the same
graph.** The current site cannot do this at all, for the same root reason it fails
everywhere: it has surfaces and views but no entities and no claims
(`TOPICAL_AUTHORITY_MAP.md` §4) — so there is no shared thing for the surfaces to
share.

### 8.2 The rule that keeps them one thing

> **A surface may render the world differently; it may never hold a fact the
> others cannot see.** The AR quest cannot know a cave the website does not. The
> wearable cannot promise a distance the ledger has not sourced. The graph is the
> single source of truth, and every surface is a window, never a warehouse.

This is the experiential form of the same discipline the sibling documents
enforce technically (`PROGRAMMATIC_SEO_PLAN.md` §5: a fact is written once). The
reason it matters *experientially* is that a person who catches two surfaces
disagreeing stops trusting both — and trust, here, is the whole product.

## 9. Current Site Review

Reviewed against the implementation: `App.tsx` (one home component, 13 sections),
`pageSections.ts`, `routes.ts`, `landingPages.ts`, `guides.ts`, `region.ts`. The
review is experiential — where a *person's* exploration breaks — not technical.

### 9.1 The shape of the current experience

The home page is a **single vertical scroll of thirteen fixed-order sections** —
hero, stats, story, legends, places, gallery, map, hub, experiences (flag off),
stay (flag off), quests, ar, app (`pageSections.ts` `HOME_SECTIONS`). The
27 landing pages and the guide/business pages are, structurally, *subsets of
these same sections* re-rendered under keyword URLs (`sectionsByRoute`,
`landingPages.ts`). There are, in experiential terms, **no destinations** — only
re-croppings of one page.

### 9.2 Where the experience breaks

| # | Break | Evidence in the repo | Layer lost |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **Discovery is impossible.** No entity has a page; the named places are cards inside `story`/`places`/`map` sections. There is nowhere to *arrive*. | `region.ts` holds 9 verified entities, none with a URL; the E1 places live only as `placeIds` in a guide | L1 dies entirely |
| B2 | **The best content is entombed.** ~4000 words of rare history render into the `story` section as a modal — no URL, no headings, no way in or out. | `App.tsx` `showSection("story")` → `copy.about`; the timeline surfaces as a modal | L3 has nothing to reward with |
| B3 | **Curiosity has no exit.** The "Ъ" fact is a stat tile; it states and stops. | `stats` section | L0 → L1 carry severed |
| B4 | **The plan cannot be made.** No distance, no drive time, no route length anywhere. | `guides.ts` deliberately omits distances (honest ✅ but leaves L4 empty); `region.ts` derives only straight-line | L4 blank |
| B5 | **Wonder replaced by adjective.** Landing-page bodies are generic ("spring and autumn are strongest"). | `landingPages.ts` — 25 pages share three section bodies | L2 flattened |
| B6 | **No mouth.** No contribution mechanism; the site only broadcasts. | no route for corrections; policy without a form | L6 impossible |
| B7 | **Nothing unfinished is shown.** The site presents as complete, so memory holds no reason to return. | complete-looking home scroll; guides marked "in preparation" is the *only* visible seam (✅, and it is the best instinct on the site) | L7 → L8 severed |

### 9.3 Where the experience repeats

The single largest experiential defect. **25 of the 27 landing pages render the
same three section bodies under different names** (`PROGRAMMATIC_SEO_PLAN.md` §0;
`landingPages.ts` has real overrides for only `lukovitGuide`, partially). A person
who follows two internal links lands twice on the same page wearing a different
title. The seam is *felt*, and every time it is felt the trust built in §5 cracks.
The internal-link graph makes this worse: it is a **star pointing at the thinnest
pages** — `visitAglen`, `weekendInAglen`, `accommodationNearAglen` receive the
most links (`INTERNAL_LINKING_GRAPH.md` §0) — so the exploration keeps funnelling
the reader *toward* the emptiest rooms.

### 9.4 Where pages behave like SEO instead of exploration

| Symptom | What it should be instead |
|---|---|
| 27 keyword pages (`natureTourism`, `ecoTourism`, `slowTravel`, `culturalTourism`… ×14 languages) | Marketing categories, not subjects. Eighteen have no referent and should be retired (`ENTITY_PRIORITY_MATRIX.md` §4). A category is not a place a person can explore |
| `aiAnswerHub` — an "answer hub" with 0 inbound links | An answer hub that answers nothing is a keyword. The real thing is a browsable source ledger |
| Navigation labelled by keyword (`attractions`, `things-to-do`) | Navigation should name the *world* — the karst, the places, the history — not the query |
| `historyOfAglen` landing page separate from the actual history text | The page named for the content does not contain the content; the content is in a modal elsewhere |

### 9.5 Where navigation feels mechanical

The routes are a flat keyword list (`routes.ts` `coreRoutes` + `landingPages`),
and every route is a *crop of the home scroll* (`sectionsForRoute`). Moving
between them feels like scrolling the same page to different anchors — because
that is literally what it is (`pageSections.ts`: "Every route here used to render
the entire home page and merely scroll to a section"). There is no sense of
*travel* through a connected place, only of re-framing one page. Navigation that
is a table of contents for a single document cannot feel like exploring a region.

### 9.6 Where curiosity dies

Precisely: **at minute 2.** A person arrives (often for Проходна or via an AR
quest), meets the "Ъ" fact or a hero image, feels a flicker of curiosity — and
then finds that every path leads back into the same vertical scroll or a
near-duplicate landing page. There is no entity to discover, no connection to
follow, no plan to make. The flicker has nowhere to go, so it goes out. The site
is *technically excellent and experientially inert* — the exact inversion the
`CONTENT_GAP_ANALYSIS.md` §4 describes as "an unusually strong sense of what it
does not know, and has not yet gone and found out."

### 9.7 What is already right and must be protected

A review that only lists breaks would misrepresent where this project stands.

| Already right | Why it is an *experiential* asset |
|---|---|
| **It refuses to guess** (`guides.ts` omits unverified distances; 4 of 6 guides openly say "in preparation") | This is the trust foundation of §5 and the only visible seam of §9.2 B7. It is the single best instinct on the site. Protect it against every pressure to fill the page |
| **`region.ts` is honest about geometry** (rivers marked `linear`, no fake distances) | The reader can feel care they cannot name |
| **The business directory has a publication state machine** (`guides.ts`, `localBusinesses`) | The one part of the site that already treats a real-world thing as an entity |
| **The AR quests exist** (✅ `quests`/`ar`/`app` sections) | A working L5 surface, waiting to be joined to the graph |
| **The "in preparation" label** | The seam that could become the site's honesty made visible |

### 9.8 Concrete recommendations (experience-level)

Not implementation. Each restores a broken layer.

1. **Give every named place a destination.** The moment Дупката, Слончето,
   Въловата дупка are things a person can *arrive at* rather than mentions,
   L1 (Discovery) exists for the first time. This is the master switch.
2. **Free the history from the modal.** The ~4000 words become the reward at the
   end of the history loop (§4.2). Zero writing; maximum L3.
3. **Turn every orientation fact into a door.** The "Ъ" fact must open onto the
   etymology, which opens onto Селището. No fact ends on a full stop.
4. **Retire the near-duplicates.** Eighteen keyword pages give the reader nothing
   to explore and repeatedly break trust at the seam. One real place beats
   eighteen labels.
5. **Replace adjectives with observations.** Every "beautiful"/"strongest" is a
   dead L2; every "lit at 07:15" is a live one. This is a writing discipline, not
   a build.
6. **Open a mouth.** A visible, credited contribution route turns readers into
   sources and gives the return loop its deepest hook (L6).
7. **Show the seams.** Make incompleteness visible — the unreached path, the
   unseen season, the stated unknown — so memory always holds a reason to return.
8. **Make navigation name the world, not the query.** The reader should feel they
   are moving through the karst, not scrolling one document to its anchors.

Recommendations 1–4 and 7 require no field work and little writing; they are
re-architecture of what already exists. That matches the `5_YEAR_SEO_ROADMAP.md`
finding: the fortnight of publication-and-retirement is worth more than the six
months after it.

## 10. Experience Principles

The experience constitution. Exactly twenty, numbered, immutable. Every future
feature — every page, quest, companion answer, map layer, wearable whisper — must
satisfy all twenty, or it does not ship. They are written to be *falsifiable*:
each can be checked against a real feature and found to pass or fail.

1. **Convert demand into connection.** Every person who arrives for a famous
   thing must be given, along a true relation, an unfamiliar thing only this site
   holds. A feature that satisfies a query and offers no onward connection has
   failed its one job.

2. **The subject is the karst; the village is the narrator.** No experience may
   re-establish the village as the root of the world. Aglen is the voice, not the
   container.

3. **Never end on a full stop.** Every fact, place, story and answer ends on an
   edge to a next thing. A dead end is where exploration becomes browsing.

4. **Specificity over adjective, always.** "The east wall is in shadow until
   ~09:30 in October" ships; "great for photography" does not. If a sentence is
   true of everywhere, it belongs nowhere.

5. **Discovery must be possible.** Every named place, person, legend and period is
   a thing a person can *arrive at*, not merely a mention inside something else.

6. **The refusal to guess is the signature, not the limitation.** When the site
   does not know, it says so, with the same prominence as what it knows, and says
   who might. This survives every pressure to fill the page.

7. **Safety and unknowns are never hidden.** A concealed hazard is the one
   unforgivable failure. A stated unknown is a strength and an invitation.

8. **Every claim carries its source and its date.** Nothing an experience asserts
   as fact may lack provenance a person — or an AI — can check.

9. **A legend is told, never asserted as true.** The mystery is the asset;
   overclaiming it destroys it. Modern inventions (Пазителят) are never mixed with
   collected tradition.

10. **The famous introduces the local.** Every experience that touches an E5
    entity (Проходна, Деветашка, Крушуна, Ловеч) must, along a real relation,
    hand the reader an E1 entity only this site documents.

11. **Loops end on new ground.** A path that returns the person to where they
    started is a browse. Every loop delivers an entity the person did not know
    existed when they entered it.

12. **Incompleteness is shown, not hidden.** The unreached path, the unseen
    season, the not-yet-known — visible always, because memory needs an unfinished
    edge to become a return.

13. **The site listens.** Every experience has a mouth: a credited, visible way
    for a person to add what they know or correct what is wrong. Belonging is
    impossible without it.

14. **One world, many windows.** Website, mobile, AR, maps, companion, wearable
    render the same graph. No surface holds a fact the others cannot see. A person
    who catches two surfaces disagreeing loses trust in both.

15. **The AI narrates only sourced claims.** The companion states unknowns, names
    who to ask, and never invents a distance, a safety rule or an access fact. An
    inventing companion is a chatbot, and a chatbot breaks six layers of trust at
    once.

16. **Distance is honest about its kind.** Straight-line and road distances are
    never blurred; a road distance without a source is not shown. The person must
    feel the honesty, not merely benefit from it.

17. **Depth is earned by sequence, never locked.** Nothing is withheld behind a
    gate, but meaning is revealed along the loops — the flint workshops mean
    "industrial prehistory" only after the legend has raised the question.

18. **Minute 1 gives a feeling; minute 5 gives a discovery; minute 20 gives a
    connection.** A visit that fails to reach discovery has failed, no matter how
    much it explained.

19. **Presence is respected.** When a person is standing in the canyon, the
    experience is quiet — a prompt at the entity, never a feed demanding the
    attention the place has earned.

20. **The reference is built by return.** The experience is designed to change,
    to say how it changed, and to bring people back to a deeper version of the
    same place. A brochure is finished; a reference is alive. This site is a
    reference.

---

*These twenty are the test. A feature that ranks well, converts well and reads
well but fails any one of them is not shipped, because it would make the
experience a tourism website again — and the whole point of this document is that
it must never be one.*
