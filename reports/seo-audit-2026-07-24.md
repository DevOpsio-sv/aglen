# SEO, performance and structured-data audit — 24 July 2026

Scope: the whole project. Everything in "Fixed" below is implemented in this
working tree, with `tsc` clean, the build green and `npm run i18n:audit` at zero
issues. Eight page types were checked in a headless browser before and after.

Numbers are measured, not estimated. Where a claim could not be verified it says so.

### 1.6 Content added

- **`src/trustPages.ts`** — the four EEAT pages were routes with metadata that
  rendered the home page. They now say something: who maintains the site, where
  each *kind* of fact comes from (Wikidata vs. local memory vs. owner-supplied),
  what is deliberately not published, how listings are reviewed, that nothing is
  paid for, how to report an error, and the crawling/citation policy. Bulgarian
  and English written out; other languages fall back to English, the same rule
  `guides.ts` and `localBusinesses.ts` already use.
- **`src/region.ts`** — the regional cluster as an entity graph: Проходна,
  Карлуково, Луковит, Искър–Панега, Златна Панега, река Вит, Деветашка пещера,
  Крушунски водопади, Ловеч, Област Ловеч. Each carries its **Wikidata id, both
  Wikipedia articles and Wikidata's coordinates**. This feeds three things at
  once: `sameAs` on real entities Google already knows, `nearbyAttraction` on the
  village, and a visible "Наблизо в Луковитския карст" block on guide pages and
  the location page with straight-line distances.
- New schema types where they are true: `CollectionPage` on index routes,
  `TouristTrip` with an `itinerary` on the five itinerary landing pages,
  `ImageObject` with `representativeOfPage`, `AdministrativeArea` for the province.

### 1.7 Tooling fixed along the way

`scripts/i18n-audit.mjs` reported three Polish-locale references that did not
exist: JavaScript's `\b` is not Unicode-aware, so `\bpl\b` matched inside
"plăteau" and "táplálva". Digits had to be excluded too, or the content hash in a
font filename reads as a locale reference. The check still catches what it is for.

## 3. Estimated impact

Honest framing: nothing here creates demand. It removes things that were actively
holding the site back and makes what exists eligible to rank.

**Large, fairly quick (weeks)**
- Killing ~390 duplicate/empty URLs concentrates ranking signals on ~590 real
  ones. Near-duplicates at that ratio suppress a whole site, not just the
  duplicates.
- Fix 23 (hreflang + schema wiped on detail pages) means Google's *rendered* view
  of every business and guide page currently has no `LocalBusiness` or
  `TravelGuide` markup and wrong alternates. Correct markup should make business
  pages eligible for local rich results for the first time.
- The 10.6 km coordinate error was pointing map results at the wrong valley.
  Anything driven by proximity was mis-anchored.

**Large, slower (1–3 months)**
- LCP: a 2.4 MB PNG plus two font handshakes almost certainly failed the 2.5 s
  threshold on 4G. 125 KB + zero external requests should pass comfortably.
  *This needs confirming with field data — see 5.*
- `sameAs` to Wikidata/Wikipedia is the strongest available signal for tying this
  site to the Проходна / Карлуково / Луковит entities. That is what "topical
  authority for the region" is made of.

**Uncertain**
- Google Discover needs original reporting and freshness; the events page is the
  only real candidate.
- AI-assistant visibility: `llms.txt`, the citation policy and clean entity
  markup all help, but no one can promise placement.

### 3.1 One regression I introduced

The JS bundle grew **835 → 1 029 KB (265 → 328 KB gzipped)** — my prose in
`trustPages.ts` and `region.ts` (Cyrillic costs two bytes a character), plus the
generated manifests. It is real and I have not fixed it; the fix belongs with the
larger problem below, which is the top item on the roadmap.

**The dominant cost is not my addition.** `src/locales/` is 469 KB of source and
*every visitor downloads all 14 languages to read one*. Converting
`contentByLanguage` to a per-language dynamic import in `main.tsx` — the
prerenderer keeps the synchronous map, since it runs in Node — should cut the
initial JS by roughly 60%. I did not attempt it in this pass: it touches every
module that imports locale content and I could not verify it as carefully as the
rest.

## 4. Remaining content work — what is still needed to own this region

**This is the biggest remaining gap, and it is writing, not code.**

**4.1 The landing pages are templated.** 25 of the 27 pages in
`landingPages.ts` share three section headings, three bodies and three FAQs;
only the page name changes. Across 14 languages that is ~350 thin pages. Two are
genuinely written (`lukovitGuide`, and the five regional guides have real
"why visit / how Aglen fits / season" text). Options, in order of preference:

1. Write real content for the 8–10 with actual search demand (Проходна,
   Карлуково, Искър–Панега, weekend from Sofia, best time, how to get there,
   family trip, camping) and `noindex` the rest until they are written.
2. Merge the weakest into the guides that already cover the topic.
3. Leave as is — the least good option; thin templated pages at this ratio are a
   site-wide quality signal.

**4.2 Verified distances and travel times.** The single most requested fact for
"уикенд край София" and "еднодневна екскурзия" queries, and the site publishes
none, correctly, because there is no verified source. Someone needs to drive the
routes once and record: Sofia → Ъглен, Луковит → Ъглен, Ъглен → Проходна/
Карлуково/Искър–Панега/Крушуна, with road distance and realistic driving time.
`region.ts` has a place for them the moment they exist. **Highest-value hour of
work available on this project.**

**4.3 Access and safety facts.** Which caves are open without a guide, where the
paths start, where parking is, whether the church can be visited. `guides.ts`
correctly refuses to guess; four of six guides are still "в процес на подготовка"
because of it.

**4.4 Photography with real captions and dates.** The current images look
AI-generated. For a place-based site, dated photographs of the actual named
places (Дупката, Слончето, Рачков вир, Калето) are worth more than any markup.

**4.5 An author.** Everything is attributed to an organisation. One named person
with a bio, local credentials and a `Person` schema node would lift EEAT more
than anything else on this list. `Трифон Кунев` being born in Ъглен is a real
hook for the history pages.
